# Gotchas

> Traps, footguns, and open risks. **Highest-ROI page** — record the resolution,
> not just the risk. Link the source/decision that explains each.
>
> Tag every entry in its heading:
> - `ANTICIPATED` — a risk; no fix yet.
> - `RESOLVED (<milestone>)` — include the fix **and** any residual risk.
> - `OPEN` — known, hurting, unfixed.
